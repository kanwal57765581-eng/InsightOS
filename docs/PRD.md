# Product Summary

InsightOS is an Autonomous AI Data Analyst platform designed to automate the complete data analysis workflow.

Instead of requiring users to manually clean datasets, write SQL queries, build dashboards, perform statistical analysis, or prepare business reports, InsightOS completes these tasks automatically using Artificial Intelligence.

The platform combines Data Engineering, Business Intelligence, Machine Learning, Statistical Analysis, Deep Learning, and Generative AI into a single intelligent system that behaves like an experienced Senior Data Analyst.

Its purpose is not only to answer questions but also to investigate business problems, discover root causes, generate recommendations, predict future trends, and assist organizations in making data-driven decisions.

---

# Product Scope

InsightOS is an AI-powered analytics platform that automates the complete business analytics lifecycle.

The platform enables users to upload structured datasets, automatically clean and validate them, generate professional visualizations, perform statistical and machine learning analysis, identify business insights, recommend actions, and create executive reports through natural language interaction.

Users can review, modify, and approve all AI-generated outputs before exporting or sharing them.

The product is intended to reduce repetitive analytical work while improving decision quality and productivity.
